FAQ

Questions parents usually ask

Does my child need prior coding experience?

No. Students are placed according to age and current skill level.

Will the child learn only Scratch?

No. Scratch is an entry point. Students can progress to game development, Python, AI and more advanced projects.

Is this online or offline?

The pilot can support Khandwa batches as well as online learning. The final schedule depends on batch availability.

Can I cancel the membership?

This demo site assumes monthly membership. Set your final cancellation/refund policy before accepting payments.
